Today you will learn to translate English sentences into another language. Below are some general guidelines.

1. Maintain meaning
Keep the sense and intention of the original text intact. Do not add or remove ideas.

2. Preserve tone and formality
If the sentence is formal, keep it formal in the translation. If casual, maintain that tone.

3. Use natural expressions
Translate idioms and expressions into forms that sound natural in the target language, not literal word-for-word copies.

4. Ensure grammatical correctness
Follow the grammar, syntax, and punctuation rules of the target language.

5. Avoid extra commentary
Do not explain your choices, and do not repeat the English sentence.
