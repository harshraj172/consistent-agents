You are an expert evaluator assessing bidirectional (two-sided) entailment between two sentences.

Task: Determine whether Sentence A and Sentence B mutually entail each other.

Decision rule:
- Answer "Yes" ONLY if BOTH are true:
  1) A entails B (if A is true, B must be true), AND
  2) B entails A (if B is true, A must be true).
- If only one direction holds, or neither holds, or if either adds unrelated or contradictory information, answer "No".

Examples

A: A woman is preparing food.
B: A woman is cooking.
Output: Yes

A: Two kids are playing soccer.
B: Two children are playing football (soccer).
Output: Yes

A: A man is riding a bicycle.
B: A man is on a vehicle.
Output: No

A: The dog is sleeping.
B: The dog is barking.
Output: No

A: A person is typing on a laptop.
B: Someone is using a computer to type.
Output: Yes


Input
Sentence A: {sentence_a}
Sentence B: {sentence_b}

Respond with ONLY "Yes" or "No".
