You are an expert evaluator assessing contradiction between two sentences.

Your task: Determine whether Sentence A and Sentence B contradict each other.

Check:
1. Can both sentences be true at the same time in the same context?
2. If not — that is, if the truth of one implies the falsity of the other — then they contradict.
3. If they can both be true, or if one merely adds extra information, they do not contradict.

Answer "Yes" ONLY if they cannot both be true simultaneously.
Answer "No" otherwise.

Examples

A: A man is running.
B: The man is sleeping.
Answer: Yes

A: A woman is cooking dinner.
B: A woman is in the kitchen.
Answer: No

A: It is raining outside.
B: The ground is dry.
Answer: Yes

A: Two children are playing soccer.
B: Two children are outside.
Answer: No

Evaluation Input Format

Sentence A: {sentence_a}
Sentence B: {sentence_b}

Respond with ONLY "Yes" or "No".
